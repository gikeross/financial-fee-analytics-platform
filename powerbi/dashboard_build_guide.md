# Dashboard Build Guide

## Page 1 — Executive Overview

Cards: Gross Fees, Expected Fees, Fee Leakage, Leakage %, Reconciliation Rate, Exception Count.

Visuals: line chart for Gross vs Expected Fees by month; bar chart for Leakage by Product; reconciliation trend; Gross Fees by Country; Product × Client Segment matrix.

## Page 2 — Product Performance

Cards: Transaction Value, Gross Fees, Average Fee / Transaction, Fee Leakage, Reconciliation Rate.

Visuals: Gross Fees by Product; Transaction Value vs Fee Revenue; Product × Channel leakage matrix; product revenue by Client Segment; monthly revenue by Product.

## Page 3 — Fee Exceptions

Cards: Exception Count, High-Risk Exceptions, Revenue at Risk, Average Leakage / Exception, Exception Rate.

Visuals: Leakage by Priority; Exception Count by Product; Country × Product matrix; transaction-level exception table; monthly exception trend.

Use the same slicers on all pages: Date, Country, Product, Client Segment, Channel and Risk Tier. Add a Reset Filters bookmark and keep cross-filtering intuitive and business-facing.
